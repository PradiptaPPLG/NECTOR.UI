## Craft Direction: NECTOR.UI
- **Identity**: Editorial digital archive, frontend laboratory, and custom web commissioning studio.
- **Palette**: Paper warm-white (`#FAF9F5`), deep charcoal (`#141416`), restrained editorial terracotta (`#C44525`), fine hairlines (`#E8E7DF`).
- **Principles**: Discover → Inspect → Preview → Commission. Every component functions. Zero fake claims or synthetic metrics.
